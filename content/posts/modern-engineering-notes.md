# Engineering Notes

This site is a **professional engineering notebook** rather than a generic personal blog.

I work at the boundary between computational engineering, simulation, automation, and software systems. The goal is to document decisions, reusable methods, experiments, and things that failed just as clearly as things that worked.

## What belongs here

- CAE and numerical simulation workflows
- Engineering software and automation
- System architecture and developer tooling
- Reproducible experiments and technical notes
- Selected project case studies

The content source is Markdown. Publishing should remain boring: add or update a `.md` file, push it, and let CI rebuild the static site.

## Principle

> Build systems that are easy to understand, easy to reproduce, and hard to lose.
